    Repeat him the class laughs

    Right after he says something, press R / ECHO. The class laughs and he walks down the rows to find who said it. Do it three times in a row and he knows it was you.

    Dumb questions he looks them up

    T / ASK. He stares at you, doesn't know the answer, and walks to his laptop to look it up. While he types, he isn't watching. Ask too many and you're in trouble.

    He gets stuck help him, or don't

    Now and then he writes a question on the board, stares at it and pats his stomach: "The answer is... is...". Raise your hand (T / ASK). The right answer calms him and gives you TRUST, but you're his pet now. A wrong answer makes the class laugh, until he looks it up. Nobody helps: he looks it up himself, and while he types he isn't watching.

    Your phone a game, meme videos

    Hold Q / right click / PHONE. Two apps: PAPER PLANE (tap E / click / TAP to fly; crash and your phone gets LOUD) and memes (switch with T / APP). Friends send you videos and classmates hold their phone out to you. Watch them to the end, but if he sees you watching a video, he takes the phone.

    Paper balls, planes, laughs

    Hold G / PRANK: a dotted arc shows where your paper goes. Let go to throw (hold longer to throw harder). X or the mouse wheel / SWAP picks a paper ball or a paper plane (slower, glides farther, curves). Hit his back and he spins around (three hits and he knows it was you), hit his head and his glasses fly off, hit a kid and they might throw one back. If he sees your arm up, you're caught. Good pranks make the class laugh (LAUGHS): everything you do is worth more mischief, but he hears the laughing around you.

    Laser and KICK ME period 3, Free Class

    X / SWAP picks them too, G / PRANK uses them. Hold the laser on: a red dot where you look. When he sees it in front of him he chases it like a cat. Too long, or in his eyes, and he looks straight down the beam: still on, and you're caught. Hold a note while he walks past your desk with his back to you and let go to stick it on: every time he turns his back, the class laughs.

    Leave your seat period 4, Free Class

    C / SEAT slips you off your chair: crawl with WASD / the stick along the aisles. Desks and kids hide you, the dark helps; the bar shows how close he is to seeing you, and if he looks right at you when it is full, he walks you back to your desk (a strike). He notices an empty chair too. At his desk, hold E / USE: steal his glasses when he puts them down (then he only sees what is close), read his laptop, put a whoopee cushion on his chair, or (Free Class) move the clock forward so the bell rings sooner. C / SIT at your desk sits you back down.

    "Who did it?" blame, lie, confess

    When he finds the note, gets a laser in his eyes, or his search after a second hit or a second echo reaches your desk, he asks you. Point at a kid (it works unless he's sure it was you, and the kid will remember), say nothing (he watches you), or confess (he forgives you once).

    His habits chalk, glasses, clowns

    When the chalk stops, stop talking. He turns around without warning. When he wipes his glasses, he can't see you at all. Sit still too long and the class clowns in the back start making noises; he goes to find out who did it. Don't stare at your phone: while you do, he comes closer, backwards, without footsteps. The chalk stops, the lights dim, somebody breathes. Look up now and then.

    Hold your breath when he listens

    When he leans over your desk he is listening: hold B / BREATH and keep the mouse still (on a phone: don't drag the view) until he leaves. Watch the BREATH meter: if you run out of air, you gasp.

    Look again things change

    The board, a poster, a seat: things change while nobody is looking at them. And sometimes your friends ask you to look somewhere. Do it. Mostly.

    Report card grades, unlocks

    After every class he grades you, A to F. He likes the quiet ones, so an A is not a compliment. Good grades and deeds unlock things: new pranks (spitball, rubber band, water balloon: switch with X / SWAP while you aim), phone themes and a third phone app (PSST!).

    Mr. Hale's notes 12 hidden notes

    12 notes are hidden in the class. You find one by doing something (looking at the right place, a prank, a question…). The NOTEBOOK (main menu and pause menu) keeps them, with a hint for each one you are missing. They tell you what happened to Mr. Hale… and how to find the two secret endings.

    Daily class the same for everyone

    The same class for everyone in the world that day (the day picks Easy or Hard, and one thing that counts double). Your best score of the day is kept.

    Easy or Hard strikes

    Easy gives you 3 strikes. Hard plays the same, but you only get one strike, and if he actually sees you whispering or on your phone, he comes for you right away.

    Play with friends up to 4, online

    Free Class: one of you hosts, the others join with the class code (up to 4). Look at a friend and hold E / TALK to whisper to them. In the meme app, look at a friend and press R / SHOW to hold your phone out to them. Throw paper at each other too. Strikes are shared, so keep each other quiet. Online extras: a note goes round (pass it on with E), caught friends haunt the class as a ghost (1 2 3), and the end screen ranks who caused the most chaos. Most jump scares go to one of you: the others see him at that friend's desk. Raise your hand to tell on a friend (true: he goes for them and you get TRUST; not true: you lose it). When he leaves the room, the most trusted player can be in charge: walk around, write names on the board, and be at the board when he comes back.

    Hosting? keep it open

    Keep the game open on your screen until your friends are in: on a phone, a game in the background can't let anybody in. Send the code with SHARE CODE (you stay in the game) or COPY INVITE LINK. Joining takes up to a minute on slow networks; the game keeps trying by itself.
